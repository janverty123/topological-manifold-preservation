# RESEARCH JOURNAL

The research journal records **discoveries**, not every experiment.

Only update this document when an experiment changes the team's understanding of the problem.

---

## Journal Entries

### YYYY-MM-DD

#### Discovery

Describe the new scientific insight.

#### Supporting Evidence

Reference the relevant experiment(s).

#### Impact

Explain how this changes the project's understanding or future direction.

---

Examples of suitable entries:

* A hypothesis was confirmed.
* A long-standing assumption was disproven.
* A new research direction emerged.
* A significant implementation issue was permanently resolved.

Routine experiment logs belong in `EXPERIMENT_LOG.md`, not here.
